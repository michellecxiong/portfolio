I've been the user.

ABIRC Marketplace

Asian Business Institute & Resource Center — Sole UX Designer

I owned it end to end: research, usability testing, user flows, wireframes, and a maintainable content system, shipped on a Squarespace budget with no design or dev support after launch.

// SERVICE DESIGN · PROCESS REDESIGN · HIGHER EDUCATION

From Warning Email to Accountability Loop

San Diego State University — Lead Academic Advisor & Lecturer

Academic probation intervention at SDSU was a single warning email with no follow-up. I redesigned it end to end — how the data reached me, how outreach happened, and what accountability looked like once a student was "helped" — and ran it solo across a 600-student caseload.
